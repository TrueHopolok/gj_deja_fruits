# Déjà Fruits

When you come home tired, you go to bed thinking about tomorrow. But suddenly you find yourself back in the past, when you used to help your grandfather with his fruit shop. Your grandfather, who is always grumbling but loves you very much, makes you arrange his goods every day. But are you ready to help your grandfather with a task that is uninteresting to you?
                                                                                                                                                                                              
## How to play?

w/a/s/d - walking

Hold Space - see previous state

If you feel stuck, walk-through is available here.
